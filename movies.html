My Top 5 Scariest Movies

1. Weapons

This movie is about children who mysteriously disappear. I think the mystery makes it creepy.

2. The Conjuring: Last Rites

This movie has supernatural events and a scary investigation. It is a good choice for Halloween.

3. Final Destination Bloodlines

This movie is full of suspense, and you never know what will happen next.

4. Black Phone 2

This movie continues a creepy story with mystery and supernatural events.

5. Sinners

This movie mixes horror and supernatural events. I think it is an really good horror movie to watch.

My Favorite Pick

My number one pick is Weapons because the story sounds creepy and makes you want to know more on what happens later on in the movie. Everyone has different opinions about scary movies but my opinion is more on suspense in movies.
